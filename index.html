About the Department

Where academic knowledge meets practical technology.

The BCA Department provides an application-oriented education in computer applications, programming, databases, web technologies, data analytics, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

Our approach combines classroom learning with laboratory practice, projects, workshops, seminars and industry-oriented activities.
